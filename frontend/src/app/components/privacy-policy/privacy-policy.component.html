Privacy Policy

Updated: September 20, 2026


Use Your Own Node When Privacy Matters

doge.tx.taxi displays public Dogecoin chain and mempool data through the backend configured by the site operator. For independent verification and the strongest network privacy, use your own Dogecoin node and explorer.


Request Data

Normal website and API requests can reveal standard web metadata to the operator, including your IP address, user agent, requested URL, and request time. Server logs may be retained for reliability, abuse prevention, and debugging.


Local Preferences

Display preferences such as theme, denomination, fiat currency, language, and timezone may be stored in your browser. doge.tx.taxi does not require an account for ordinary explorer use.


Public Blockchain Data

Dogecoin transactions, addresses, scripts, and blocks are public. Looking up an address or transaction can associate that lookup with your network metadata. Do not treat a public explorer as a privacy boundary.


External Links

This site links to tx.taxi contact channels, Dogecoin documentation, and upstream source code. Those external services have their own privacy policies.
