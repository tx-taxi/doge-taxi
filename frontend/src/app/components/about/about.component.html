About doge.tx.taxi

doge.tx.taxi is the Dogecoin explorer in the tx.taxi multi-chain family. It preserves Mempool's proven interface for inspecting blocks, transactions, addresses, mining activity, fee estimates, and the live Dogecoin mempool while using a Dogecoin-specific tx.taxi identity.

Bitcoin-native data

Values are denominated in DOGE, koinu, or the selected fiat currency. Fee rates remain koinu/B, transaction sizes remain B or weight units, and all block, address, mining, concepts shown by the interface retain their Dogecoin meaning.

MWEB coverage

This explorer shows transparent-chain transactions and indexed addresses. Private MWEB amounts and recipient history are not publicly observable. HogEx transactions connect the extension block to the transparent chain.

Infrastructure

The explorer reads public Dogecoin network data through its configured backend. Explorer results are informational and can differ from another node's view of the mempool. Always verify payment details in your wallet and use your own node when independent verification matters.
