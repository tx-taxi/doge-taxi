About doge.tx.taxi

doge.tx.taxi is the Dogecoin explorer in the tx.taxi multi-chain family. It preserves Mempool's proven interface for inspecting blocks, transactions, addresses, mining activity, fee estimates, and observed pending Dogecoin transactions while using a Dogecoin-specific tx.taxi identity.

Dogecoin-native data

Values are denominated in DOGE, koinu, or the selected fiat currency. Fee rates remain koinu/B, transaction sizes use bytes, and all block, address, mining, concepts shown by the interface retain their Dogecoin meaning.

Provider coverage

Pending transactions are an observed sample, not a complete global mempool. Historical mining series remain unavailable from the current provider. Indexed address history depends on provider access and may be throttled. Rate limits can temporarily prevent uncached data from loading.

Infrastructure

The explorer reads public Dogecoin network data through its configured backend. Explorer results are informational and can differ from another node's view of the mempool. Always verify payment details in your wallet and use your own node when independent verification matters.
